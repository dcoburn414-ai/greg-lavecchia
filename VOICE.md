# VOICE

## Summary
Founder-operator CPG voice: candid, high-energy, occasionally profane in podcasts, story-heavy. Mixes dorm-room / bootstrapped origin grit with boardroom distribution talk (Target, Amazon, Dr Pepper trucks). Speaks in first person about what he and Mari/Leo actually did — meme pages, restocks, cartel truck theft Christmas, McLaren-next-to-Domino's stress — then extracts a tactical rule. Prefers concrete channel and product language over abstract motivational fog. Sounds like a marketer-CEO who still refreshes sales and thinks month-to-month.

## Patterns
- One-line claim → story from Bloom/Mari Fitness → rule you can steal
- Marketplace metaphors: arena is public, minor leagues vs major leagues, pour gasoline on the fire, sugar-daddy business funding the next SKU
- Binary reframes: too late vs too early; discount vs restock; paint job vs liquid; agency check-writing vs proprietary in-house
- Self-deprecating academic/ADHD honesty (2.4 GPA, "I can't read") paired with competitive validity obsession (Amazon #1, Target, Top energy)
- Short planning language: month-to-month, six-month world changes, don't think past a year early on
- Cadence: podcast monologues with "yeah / right / like" filler + crisp quotables for LinkedIn/IG ("restocked," "second mover," "don't recreate the wheel")
